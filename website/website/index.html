Welcome to Spain!

Spain is a country in southwestern Europe known for its beautiful cities, beaches, historic buildings, music and delicious food. Every part of Spain has something different to explore. In this travel diary, let's discover some of its most famous places and traditions.

Places to Visit

Spain has many interesting cities. Here are four places that make the country special.

1. Madrid

Madrid is the capital of Spain. It is known for its lively streets, museums, parks and the Royal Palace.

2. Barcelona

Barcelona is famous for its architecture, beaches and the amazing Sagrada Familia church.

3. Valencia

Valencia is a coastal city known for its beaches, old town and the City of Arts and Sciences.

4. Seville

Seville is known for flamenco, colourful festivals and beautiful historic buildings.

Taste of Spain

Spanish food is colourful and full of flavour. Some popular dishes include:

  • Paella: A rice dish usually made with vegetables and seafood or meat.
  • Tapas: Small dishes served as snacks or shared with friends.
  • Churros: Fried dough pastries often enjoyed with thick chocolate.

Spanish Culture

Spain has a rich culture shaped by music, dance, art and festivals. Flamenco is one of its best-known traditions. Festivals such as La Tomatina and Las Fallas bring people together with music, food and celebrations.

Spanish is the main language, and regional languages and traditions are also important in different parts of the country.

Why is Spain Special?

Spain is special because it combines history, modern life, natural beauty and different regional traditions. Its historic monuments, art, architecture and welcoming festivals attract visitors from around the world.

Football in Spain

Football is one of the most popular sports in Spain. The country is home to famous clubs such as Real Madrid and FC Barcelona. Spanish teams and players have also made a strong mark in international football.
